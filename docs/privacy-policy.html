Privacy Policy for Artfiles

Effective Date: October 15, 2026
Application: Artfiles (com.artraw.files)
Developer: rawatriya7514-lab

Core Privacy Pledge: Artfiles is designed as a privacy-first file management application. All file indexing, searching, audio/video playback, image viewing, and ZIP archive operations occur strictly on your local device. We never upload, transmit, or sell your files, photos, or documents to remote servers.

1. Device Storage & Permissions

To provide file management functions, Artfiles requests access to your device storage:

  • All Files Access (MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE): Required strictly to inspect, organize, rename, delete, move, and compress files across your internal and external storage cards.
  • Media Permissions (READ_MEDIA_IMAGES, READ_MEDIA_VIDEO, READ_MEDIA_AUDIO): Used to populate the categorized dashboards and built-in offline media players.
  • Package Inspection (REQUEST_INSTALL_PACKAGES): Allows the user to inspect APK package metadata (app name, icon, version) and initiate manual installation upon explicit user confirmation.

2. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

To support free development and continuous updates, Artfiles integrates the official Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob). Google AdMob may automatically collect and process certain device data, including:

  • Google Advertising ID (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID)
  • IP address (for coarse geographic location and fraud prevention)
  • Device model, manufacturer, operating system version, and screen resolution
  • Interaction data (ad impressions, clicks, video completion for rewarded ads)

This data is processed in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy. You can learn how Google uses data from apps that use its services by visiting How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

3. User Choices & Ad Settings

You have full control over your advertising identifiers and personalized ads:

  • You can opt out of personalized ads or reset your Advertising ID at any time via your Android device settings: Settings → Google → Ads.
  • You can manage your Google Ad settings globally at Google Ads Settings.

4. Child Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Artfiles does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13. The application is configured with Google Mobile Ads setTagForChildDirectedTreatment(false) and general audience content ratings.

5. Authorized Digital Sellers (app-ads.txt)

In compliance with IAB Tech Lab and Google AdMob standards, our official Authorized Digital Sellers verification file is published at app-ads.txt.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions regarding this Privacy Policy or the security of Artfiles, please open an issue on our GitHub repository: rawatriya7514-lab/artraw-files.
